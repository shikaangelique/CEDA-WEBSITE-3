# CEDA Homepage Quote Deck

Verified, source-attributed quotes from named figures in Ghana's and Africa's extractives, energy, and governance space. For use on the CEDA landing page.

## Samuel Bekoe
*Executive Director, Centre for Extractives and Development Africa (CEDA)*

> "The public interest case for major spending from mineral revenues demands transparency, not just intent."

Source: Hot Digital Online and B&FT, 18 March 2026. CEDA and NRGI media training session, Accra.

---

> "Because these resources are non-renewable and exhaustible, if we do not invest them very well, our future generations will come and be depressed."

Source: Adom Online, 18 March 2026.

## Benjamin (Ben) Boakye
*Executive Director, Africa Centre for Energy Policy (ACEP)*

> "What Ghana needs is the enforcement of contractual obligations, not the acquisition of non-performing assets. There is too much poverty in this country for the state to spend scarce public funds on wasteful, trumped-up ventures."

Source: GhanaWeb and The Herald Ghana, 20 to 21 November 2025.

---

> "What drives me is the conviction in the potential to reduce poverty through policy reforms, transparency and accountability in the governance of resources."

Source: OGEL Journal contributing author profile.

*Co-Chair, Ghana Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative (GHEITI)*

Source: Africa Renewal, United Nations, 2012. Reprinted by AllAfrica.

---

> "If we don't monitor, we risk corruption, inefficiencies, and misplaced priorities."

Source: Ghana News Agency, 26 March 2025.
